# Red-Beard Weather

Weather at a glance inside Obsidian. The user should recognize current conditions and understand the next few hours within seconds, with the week ahead immediately available in the full view.

Both the dashboard widget and standalone view use the same government data. The widget is a concise reading surface, rather than a miniature settings or diagnostics page. The full view uses graphics and summaries; visible hourly tables are excluded by user preference.

Keep direct NWS requests, offline caching, commercial-friendly licensing, snapshots, existing commands, theme integration, and mobile compatibility. No new service, paid provider, or runtime graphics dependency. Preserve meaningful stale/missing-data and alert uncertainty states. Full station provenance and technical errors belong in secondary details or settings, not the widget.
